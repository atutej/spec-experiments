# [drafting] DSpark drafters for Snowball (Grug-67B-A2B): itw vs targetgen training, serving, and RL status

Related: #9079 (EAGLE-3 PoC), #9114 (adapted EAGLE-3 draft), #9312 (drafting tracker), #9735 (Snowball broad-domain RLVR).

## 1. Overview

We trained two DSpark drafters for [`open-athena/Grug-67B-A2B-Datakit-SFT-262K-2026.09.21`](https://huggingface.co/open-athena/Grug-67B-A2B-Datakit-SFT-262K-2026.09.21) and measured them in vLLM against plain decoding. Both have the same architecture and recipe (3 decoder layers, hidden 2560, FFN 3840, block size 8, 32k draft vocabulary, 280M parameters). They differ in the text they train on, which we call *itw* and *targetgen*:

| | itw drafter | targetgen drafter |
|---|---|---|
| Training text | the gold replies of the Datakit SFT mix (the data the target was fine-tuned on) | the target's own replies (T=1) to 270k sampled Datakit conversations |
| Training length | 80,813 steps, about 9.7B tokens, 21% of one epoch (stopped at a time limit; learning rate not yet decayed) | 17,737 steps, 2.1B tokens, one epoch (learning rate decayed to zero) |
| Compute (2 GB200 nodes) | about 11.7 h of training | 10.2 h of generation + 3.0 h of merge and training |

**Naming.** *itw* (in-the-wild) means the drafter trains on text that already exists, here the replies in the Datakit SFT mix (earlier notes and run names call this "offline" or "off-policy"). *targetgen* (target-generated) means it trains on the target model's own regenerated replies (earlier: "on-policy" or "online").

Main findings:

- Both drafters speed up Snowball decoding: about 1.8x to 2.2x output tokens/s over plain decoding at concurrency 16 (1.5x on later-turn Datakit prompts), with acceptance lengths of 2.8 to 3.1 (2.4 to 2.6 on later turns) (Section 3.1).
- The itw drafter is ahead of the targetgen drafter on every set we measured, by about 0.3 in acceptance length. One epoch of targetgen data did not make up for having 4.6 times fewer training tokens.
- Both drafters transfer to the Dr. Doom fine-tune of the target (acceptance falls by 0.4 to 0.5; the speed-up over plain decoding stays at 1.6x to 1.8x) (Section 3.1).
- The drafter loads in the pinned Marin vLLM fork unchanged. It needs `draft_sample_method: probabilistic` and `rejection_sample_method: standard` set in the serving config for sampled decoding; these are not stored in the checkpoint (Section 4).
- The trainer's validation `accept_len` overstates serving acceptance by about 0.6 to 0.9 tokens, mainly because serving chains its rounds (Section 3.2).
- For RL: the split layout (policy and engines on separate nodes) ran one full RL step with the drafter intact. `colocate_all` does not work with a draft today (acceptance falls to 1.0). The MarinSkyRL change that accepts `dspark` in the managed config is on a [fork branch](https://github.com/atutej/MarinSkyRL/tree/dspark-speculative-decoding) and has not yet been run for several steps (Section 3.4).

## 2. Methodology

### 2.1 Target and drafter

- Target: [`open-athena/Grug-67B-A2B-Datakit-SFT-262K-2026.09.21`](https://huggingface.co/open-athena/Grug-67B-A2B-Datakit-SFT-262K-2026.09.21) (`grug_moe`, hidden 2560, 26 layers, 256 experts), served by vLLM's [`grugmoe.py`](https://github.com/marin-community/vllm/blob/39e62869693c46402b1a95fde4fc55ca1aab9ae1/vllm/model_executor/models/grugmoe.py).
- Drafter: DSpark (DFlash plus a Markov logit-bias head and a confidence head), added to vLLM in [vllm-project/vllm#46995](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/46995); the draft model class is [`qwen3_dspark.py`](https://github.com/marin-community/vllm/blob/39e62869693c46402b1a95fde4fc55ca1aab9ae1/vllm/model_executor/models/qwen3_dspark.py). The architecture is set by [`draft_config.json`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/draft_config.json) and the training recipe by [`settings.sh`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/settings.sh):
  - 3 `qwen3`-type layers, FFN 3840 (1.5 x hidden), 20 heads / 5 KV heads, block size 8;
  - hidden states tapped from target layers 2, 12, 22 plus the final layer;
  - 32,000-token draft vocabulary built from the supervised-token frequencies of the training text; the drafter's `lm_head` equals the target's `lm_head` rows for those tokens, bit for bit (checked on both drafters);
  - Markov head rank 256 (vanilla), confidence head; loss 0.1 CE + 0.9 TV against the target's distribution; learning rate 3e-4; 12,288 anchors per 32,768-token sequence.
- Training code: our fork of vllm-project/speculators, branch [`grug-dspark-gate`](https://github.com/atutej/speculators/tree/grug-dspark-gate), with three changes on top of upstream: Grug's gated final norm in the DFlash/DSpark targets and `grug_moe` registration ([f93d1a5](https://github.com/atutej/speculators/commit/f93d1a57998f552a716322925c19eae8d9202342)), `launch_vllm.py` reading the layer count from `config.json` ([96699f9](https://github.com/atutej/speculators/commit/96699f9ad8740040ed18cf2a9443ec33dd740dad)), and HF-style step checkpoints ([99a50bf](https://github.com/atutej/speculators/commit/99a50bf3977776a4a0f5548e5a25e28e6cdd396a)). Not upstream.

Decisions and the Marin commit choice are in [`PLAN.md`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/PLAN.md).

### 2.3 Training setup (both experiments)

Hidden states are extracted online, as in the [Qwen3-0.6B runs](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_qwen3_0_6b_nemotron-terminal-corpus_itw/NOTES.md): a vLLM server runs the target and hands hidden states to the trainer through [Mooncake](https://github.com/kvcache-ai/Mooncake), so nothing is stored on disk. On each of 2 Vista GB200 nodes, 2 GPUs serve the target (TP=1, DP2 x EP2) next to their own `mooncake_master`, and 2 GPUs train; the 4 trainer ranks run as one DDP job. A step is about 120,000 tokens and takes 0.52 s. The entry points are [`vista/run_2node_ddp.sh`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/vista/run_2node_ddp.sh), [`vista/run_2node_ddp.sbatch`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/vista/run_2node_ddp.sbatch) and [`vista/submit_chain.sh`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/vista/submit_chain.sh) (a chain of resumable 12 h jobs). Metrics go to W&B project [`atutej/marin_speculator`](https://wandb.ai/atutej/marin_speculator).

### 2.4 Experiment A: itw drafter

Train on the dataset as written: the gold Datakit replies stand in for the target's completions. One epoch would be 382,747 steps (45.95B tokens); the run was stopped by the time limit at step 80,813 (about 9.7B tokens), so the learning rate (linear decay over the full epoch) had not annealed. The checkpoint we evaluate is `checkpoint-80813`. W&B runs for the three resumed segments: [jgsst5k0](https://wandb.ai/atutej/marin_speculator/runs/jgsst5k0), [71rm9vy8](https://wandb.ai/atutej/marin_speculator/runs/71rm9vy8), [ij7oistz](https://wandb.ai/atutej/marin_speculator/runs/ij7oistz). The trainer's validation `accept_len` at step 80,813 (3.785) is recorded in [`NOTES.md`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/NOTES.md).

### 2.5 Experiment B: targetgen drafter

Same dataset, drafter and recipe, but the supervised text is the target's own. The experiment folder is [`dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_targetgen`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/tree/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_targetgen). It is a chain of two 2-node jobs submitted by `vista/submit_chain.sh`:

1. **Regenerate** (`vista/run_regen.sh` with [`regenerate_token_rows.py`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/regenerate_token_rows.py)). 270,000 rows (4.2% of the train split) are sampled uniformly without replacement. Each node runs its own vLLM (DP4 x EP4, 512 concurrent sequences) on half the rows. For each assistant turn in order, the prompt is everything before it (the original non-assistant tokens plus the target's own earlier turns), the target generates the turn at T=1, top_p=1, up to 32,768 new tokens, stopping at `<|eot_id|>`; the generated tokens (mask 1) replace the original turn, and the original tokens that follow it (tool output, next user turn) are kept with mask 0. A row is cut at 32,768 tokens in total.
2. **Merge and train.** The parts are merged into an Arrow dataset (shuffled), the 32k draft vocabulary is rebuilt from the target's own regenerated tokens (it covers 98.06% of them), and the same 2-node training runs one epoch. The itw run's `run_2node_ddp.sh` is reused with this experiment's settings. The merged dataset has 2.13B tokens. W&B run: [6wnqmuwi](https://wandb.ai/atutej/marin_speculator/runs/6wnqmuwi). The final validation metrics (`accept_len` 3.724) are in [`targetgen_trainer_val_metrics.json`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/results/dspark_eval/targetgen_trainer_val_metrics.json).

Regeneration statistics, from the final lines of the two regeneration logs ([`targetgen_jobs_and_regeneration.txt`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/results/dspark_eval/targetgen_jobs_and_regeneration.txt)): 1.229B generated tokens (about 4,550 per row), 0 errors, 5.0% of rows ended on the 32k per-turn cap, 0.4% ran into the window limit; generation ran at 16.7k to 19.2k tokens/s per node, averaged over the run. In a 3,000-row pilot, 26% of rows were multi-turn (7.4 regenerated turns on average).

We used our own token-level regenerator, not the speculators `regenerate-responses` CLI, because the dataset stores token ids with a loss mask and no chat messages. Going through the CLI means decoding each row back to messages and tools ([`rows_to_messages.py`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/rows_to_messages.py); the round trip is exact on 98.4% of 500 sampled rows) and then training on one row per generation with the whole prefix repeated: 3.8 times more training tokens on a 3,000-row sample, 10.6 times for multi-turn rows ([`boundary_layout_inflation.py`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/boundary_layout_inflation.py); output and the round-trip check in [`regenerate_responses_cli_cost.txt`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/results/dspark_eval/regenerate_responses_cli_cost.txt)).

### 2.6 Evaluation

Every arm serves the target in the pinned Marin vLLM fork, [`marin-vllm-gpu-20260929-39e62869693c`](https://github.com/marin-community/vllm/releases/tag/marin-vllm-gpu-20260929-39e62869693c), the same wheel [MarinSkyRL pins](https://github.com/marin-community/MarinSkyRL/blob/8ab8f3a7b44c9828efd8c41a88d5fa2f8eab9a68/pyproject.toml#L100). Settings are `--tensor-parallel-size 1 --data-parallel-size 4 --enable-expert-parallel` on one GB200 node, one server at a time on an otherwise idle node, concurrency 16, T=1, top_p=1. The drafter arms use `{"method": "dspark", "num_speculative_tokens": 8, "draft_sample_method": "probabilistic", "rejection_sample_method": "standard"}`; plain decoding is the baseline. Concurrency 16 matches the `max_num_seqs: 16` per engine in the RL config ([`glm53_rlvr1_antidoom_reference_kl.yaml`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-athena/snowball-broad-domain-rlvr-campaign-2026/blob/main/configs/glm53_rlvr1_antidoom_reference_kl.yaml)).

- Acceptance length is `1 + accepted/drafts` from vLLM's speculative-decoding counters, using the definition in speculators' [`perf_utils.py`](https://github.com/vllm-project/speculators/blob/main/scripts/evaluate/perf_utils.py), over the whole chained rollout.
- Throughput is generated tokens divided by wall time for the whole set; the gain is the ratio to the plain-decoding arm on the same prompts.
- Harness: [`serve_eval.sh`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/eval_acceptance/serve_eval.sh) (one server per arm) and [`run_eval.py`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/eval_acceptance/run_eval.py) (prompts, sampling, counters); for batch jobs, [`eval_arms.sbatch`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/eval_acceptance/eval_arms.sbatch) and [`submit_eval.sh`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/eval_acceptance/submit_eval.sh).

Prompt sets (manifests with seeds and hashes: [`docs/results/dspark_eval/prompt_manifests/`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/tree/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/results/dspark_eval/prompt_manifests)):

- **(a) RL prompts.** Rows of the Snowball broad-domain RLVR training set, [`open-athena/Nemotron-RL-Ultra-RLVR1-Cleaned-v3-2026-09-30`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-athena/Nemotron-RL-Ultra-RLVR1-Cleaned-v3-2026-09-30) (the corrected preparation described in the campaign's [`CLEANED_DATASET.md`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-athena/snowball-broad-domain-rlvr-campaign-2026/blob/main/audits/CLEANED_DATASET.md); train split), rendered as the RL requests carry them: stored messages (including earlier assistant tool calls and tool outputs), each row's tool schemas (the `request_json` column) passed as `tools=`, and the chat template's default `Reasoning: /think` (the [RL config](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-athena/snowball-broad-domain-rlvr-campaign-2026/blob/main/configs/glm53_rlvr1_antidoom_reference_kl.yaml) passes no `chat_template_kwargs`; the [template](https://huggingface.co/open-athena/Grug-67B-A2B-Datakit-SFT-262K-2026.09.21/blob/main/chat_template.jinja) defaults to `/think`). Generation settings follow the same config: up to 49,152 new tokens per turn, 102,400-token window, T=1. 1,024 prompts sampled at random; we ran the first 256, which are themselves a uniform sample. Mix of the 1,024: tool use 389, instruction following 148, code 94, math 55, MCQA 44, MultiChallenge 41, others; 229 prompts already contain tool-call history. Built by [`build_nemotron_rlvr_prompts.py`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/eval_acceptance/build_nemotron_rlvr_prompts.py).
- **(b) Datakit validation prompts, multi-turn.** 1,024 prompts from the 6,472 held-out Datakit rows, which neither drafter trained on. For each row a uniformly random assistant turn is chosen, with the dataset's earlier turns as context: 833 first-turn and 191 later-turn prompts (median context 3,119 tokens, longest 27,450). Replies capped at 4,096 tokens. Built by [`build_val_prompts.py --turns random`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/eval_acceptance/build_val_prompts.py).
- **(c) Dr. Doom target.** The same RL-prompt run with the target replaced by [`open-athena/Grug-67B-A2B-Datakit-SFT-262K-2026.09.21-dr-doom`](https://huggingface.co/open-athena/Grug-67B-A2B-Datakit-SFT-262K-2026.09.21-dr-doom) (the FTPO de-looping fine-tune). Both drafters were trained against the 09.21 hidden states, so this measures transfer to a fine-tuned target.

### 2.7 RL integration

MarinSkyRL (pin [`5de48d34`](https://github.com/marin-community/MarinSkyRL/commit/5de48d34), vLLM wheel as above) was run on Vista GB200 with the DSpark drafter in an R2E-Gym RL setup. The infrastructure notes are in [`docs/rl_dspark_infra.md`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/rl_dspark_infra.md) and the A/B notes in [`r2egym-rl-ab/NOTES.md`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/rl_speculation/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_r2egym-rl-ab/NOTES.md). The two layouts are [`2node_grug_datakit_dspark_r2egym_rl.yaml`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/rl_speculation/configs/2node_grug_datakit_dspark_r2egym_rl.yaml) (split, `weight_sync_transport: expert_block`) and [`…_rl_coloc.yaml`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/rl_speculation/configs/2node_grug_datakit_dspark_r2egym_rl_coloc.yaml) (`colocate_all`). The managed [`generator.speculative_decoding`](https://github.com/marin-community/MarinSkyRL/blob/8ab8f3a7b44c9828efd8c41a88d5fa2f8eab9a68/marinskyrl/speculative_decoding.py#L26-L29) block accepts only `eagle3`. ### 3.2 Trainer metric versus serving

The validation `accept_len` the trainer logs is 3.785 (itw, step 80,813, on Datakit validation rows; [`NOTES.md`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/NOTES.md)) and 3.724 (targetgen, final, on its own held-out rows; [`targetgen_trainer_val_metrics.json`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/results/dspark_eval/targetgen_trainer_val_metrics.json)). Serving gives less, for two reasons we could separate:

- The trainer averages the accepted length of one draft round started at each of many positions, equally weighted. Serving chains rounds: after a long accepted run it skips an easy stretch, after a short one it stays in the hard spot, so its number is closer to a harmonic mean. Measured with the same text, a single round from uniformly random positions in the target's own replies gives 3.642 (itw) and 3.271 (targetgen) ([raw probe results](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/tree/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/results/dspark_eval/random_anchor_probe_target_replies)), against 3.122 and 2.826 chained on the same Datakit validation prompts with probabilistic drafting ([raw results](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/tree/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/results/dspark_eval/datakit_val_firstturn_1024_probabilistic)). On the targetgen run's own held-out rows (long multi-turn contexts, supervised anchors) the one-round numbers are 3.855 and 3.501 ([raw probe results](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/tree/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/results/dspark_eval/random_anchor_probe_heldout_targetgen_rows)).
- Greedy drafting costs about 0.3 more than probabilistic drafting (for the itw drafter 2.660 against 2.973 on an earlier 128-prompt set; see [`NOTES.md`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/experiments/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft_datakit-sft-mix_itw/NOTES.md)).

Teacher-forced parity between the trainer's drafter and vLLM's drafter was checked: they pick the same token in 97 to 98% of slots, with equal acceptance on common anchors, so we found no implementation mismatch (also in `NOTES.md`). One residual remains unexplained: on its own held-out rows the targetgen drafter reaches 3.50 in the one-round probe against the trainer's 3.72. The probe is [`probe_random_anchor.py`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/eval_acceptance/probe_random_anchor.py) with the launcher [`probe_serve.sh`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/speculator_training/tools/eval_acceptance/probe_serve.sh).

### 3.4 RL status

- **Split layout** (policy on one node, one DP4 x EP4 engine on another, [`weight_sync_transport: expert_block`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/rl_speculation/configs/2node_grug_datakit_dspark_r2egym_rl.yaml), Triton MoE backend): one RL step ran end to end with the drafter intact (accept length 2.54 in the step-1 rollouts; the weight sync after training took 1.5 s). The default broadcast sync ran out of memory in this layout. The run failed later in a way we have not diagnosed, so we do not yet have acceptance after a weight sync. Details: [`docs/rl_dspark_infra.md`](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/docs/rl_dspark_infra.md).
- **`colocate_all`** ([config](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/rl_speculation/configs/2node_grug_datakit_dspark_r2egym_rl_coloc.yaml)): the draft did not survive. Accept length was 1.000 in both runs. Engines sleep during training, and a [probe](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/pipelines/rl_speculation/tools/sleep_wake_probe.py) showed that sleep level 2 discards the draft's weights; our level-1 [patch](https://github.com/atutej/spec-experiments/blob/91e20d39f5f4bc62f956d6a9e0e67b7dcbb1ab74/setup/machines/vista/patches/marinskyrl-sleep-level.patch) is not confirmed in an RL run. The managed `speculative_decoding` block rejects `colocate_all=true` at launch; our colocated runs passed `speculative_config` through the engine kwargs directly, which bypasses that check.
- **Draft head after weight sync:** MarinSkyRL re-slices the draft's `lm_head` from the updated policy after each sync, but only for [`eagle3`](https://github.com/marin-community/MarinSkyRL/blob/8ab8f3a7b44c9828efd8c41a88d5fa2f8eab9a68/skyrl-train/skyrl_train/inference_engines/vllm/vllm_engine.py#L594), using vLLM's [`refresh_target_owned_draft_weights`](https://github.com/marin-community/vllm/blob/39e62869693c46402b1a95fde4fc55ca1aab9ae1/vllm/v1/spec_decode/online_eagle.py#L89). The DSpark drafter's head is the same kind of frozen slice (bit-identical to the target's rows), so [the fork branch](https://github.com/atutej/MarinSkyRL/commit/9a0ea386eaf6ccc35391ebe3fba2d5744a3f2cb6) extends the refresh to `dspark`. The refresh has not been exercised in a multi-step RL run.
- The drafter is not trained during RL, so acceptance is expected to decay as the policy moves away from the 09.21 weights.

### 3.5 Limitations

- We have not run RL with these drafters yet; Section 3.4 is the state of that work.
- In MarinSkyRL, colocating the draft with the policy (`colocate_all`) currently disables it: acceptance falls to 1.0.
