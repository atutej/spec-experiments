# Datakit-orig vs BigMix-TG training data: what could explain the eval differences (2026-11-14)

Scripts: this folder (datacmp.py, evalside.py, evalcopy.py, contam.py, report.py, make_decontam.py). All tables: `report_tables.md`. Raw arrays stay on Vista (`runs/data_compare_20261114/results`).
Scanned: BigMix TG (all 296,000 rows), Datakit TG (all 281,000), BigMix original pool (all 2,929,705), Datakit original (statistics: every third shard, 2,168,169 of 6,471,159 rows; overlap check: all rows). Row and token totals match the datasets' known totals.

## Findings
1. **Draft vocabulary.** Each drafter can only propose the 32,000 target tokens in its vocabulary, built from its training data's token frequencies. Datakit orig spends 3,236 slots (10%) on CJK and other non-ASCII tokens, BigMix TG 1,184 (4%); BigMix TG has 2,808 more English word pieces, Datakit orig 632 more LaTeX/code-symbol tokens (1,558 against 926). Share of the target's real eval-reply tokens a vocabulary cannot propose (Datakit orig / BigMix TG): GSM8K 2.61% / 1.29%, MT-Bench 3.49% / 2.18%, Datakit held-out 2.10% / 2.55%, Snowball replay 3.06% / 5.09%, finance 0.92% / 2.38%. Using the measured per-position acceptance profile, the coverage difference alone implies +0.125 AL for BigMix TG on GSM8K (measured gap to NoFin +0.145), +0.074 on MT-Bench (measured +0.021), -0.196 on the Snowball replay (measured -0.544), and about 0 on HumanEval, AIME, MATH-500.
2. **Repetition in the completions.** Supervised tokens inside 8-grams seen earlier in the row: Datakit orig 27.8% (6.0% from the prompt or tool output, 21.8% from earlier assistant text), BigMix TG 12.7%, Datakit TG 18.2%, BigMix orig 21.2%. The target's eval replies: Snowball replay 38.6% (35.5% copied from the prompt), GSM8K 21.4%, finance 19.7%, MATH-500 16.9%, Datakit held-out 15.0%, AIME 13.9%, BigMix held-out 11.5%, HumanEval 10.6%, MBPP 8.0%, MT-Bench 7.6%.
3. **Eval problems inside the training prompts** (13-gram coverage of at least 50%; problems checked in brackets): GSM8K (1,278) 86 Datakit orig / 5 BigMix TG; MATH-500 (393) 100 / 17; HumanEval (164) 8 / 23; MT-Bench (50) 33 / 0; AIME (59) 1 / 0. Datakit TG: 8, 14, 2, 6, 0. BigMix original pool: 18, 26, 58, 3, 0. Several examples were decoded and are verbatim or lightly reworded copies. MBPP prompts are too short to check.
4. **Reasoning mode and style.** BigMix TG is 100% `/think`; Datakit orig and Datakit TG are 63.6% `/think` rows (85-90% of supervised tokens); BigMix orig is 18.6% `/think` rows. Both TG sets carry the target's own style (`Wait` 1.8 and `Actually` 2.2-2.5 per 1k tokens, against 0.85 and 0.82 in Datakit orig). 17-18% of think blocks in the TG sets are cut off by the 32,768 window, 3.8% in Datakit orig.
5. **Terminal-agent prompts** (Terminus-2): share of supervised tokens 9.5% in BigMix TG, 3.2% Datakit orig, 2.7% Datakit TG, 26.2% BigMix orig.
6. **Not explanatory:** prompt format. Prompts asking for `\boxed` or step by step are more common in Datakit (10.6% and 8.9%) than in BigMix TG (6.7% and 3.1%).
7. **Unigram closeness** of the eval replies to each dataset's supervised `/think` text predicts the sign of the BigMix TG minus Datakit orig AL gap on 9 of 10 eval sets (exception: MATH-500).

## Caveats
Implied vocabulary effects assume the uncoverable tokens would otherwise be accepted at the average rate (an upper bound). Contamination effects on AL were not measured: no per-problem acceptance exists. Decontaminated prompt sets (problems with at least 50% coverage in any of the four datasets removed) are in `runs/paper_prompts_decontam_20261114`.

## Addendum (2026-11-14, later): format and reworded-variant checks
- **Exact eval prompt wrapper** (`Please reason step by step, and put your final answer within \boxed{}.`) at the end of the first user turn, spread sample of 9,000 to 12,000 rows: Datakit orig 2.7%, Datakit TG 2.5%, BigMix TG 0 of 9,000, BigMix orig 0 of 12,000. "step by step" in the prompt: 9.5% / 9.0% / 0.03% / 0.03%. Replies containing `\boxed{`: 17.9% / 14.7% / 15.8% / 27.4%. Format favors Datakit, so it does not explain BigMix TG's lead.
- **6-gram overlap** (finds reworded variants too), share of eval problems with a training prompt holding at least half of their 6-grams: GSM8K Datakit orig 8.4%, BigMix orig pool 1.6%, Datakit TG 0.8%, BigMix TG 0.5%. HumanEval: 5.5%, 45.1%, 1.8%, 17.1% (at least 30%: 9.8%, 70.1%, 3.0%, 29.9%). MATH-500: 31.3%, 10.4%, 4.3%, 6.4%. MT-Bench: 72.0%, 10.0%, 14.0%, 0%. AIME: 3.4%, 0%, 1.7%, 0%.
- **GSM8K lead of BigMix TG over Datakit orig (+0.247):** vocabulary coverage implies +0.117 (47%); neither contamination (Datakit has more) nor prompt format (Datakit has more) nor reworded variants (none found in BigMix TG) explain the rest.
