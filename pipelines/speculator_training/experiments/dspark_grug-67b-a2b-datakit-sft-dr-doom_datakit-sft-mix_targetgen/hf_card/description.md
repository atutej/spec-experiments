**Targetgen drafter for the Dr. Doom target, trained from random initialization on Dr. Doom's own replies.** One epoch (31,932 steps, about 3.8B tokens, 5 h 40 min on 2 GB200 nodes including the data merge) on **Dr. Doom's replies to 400,000 Datakit SFT conversations**: Dr. Doom regenerated every assistant turn of each conversation (T=1), and the drafter trained on that text with Dr. Doom's hidden states. There is no itw (existing text) stage and no warm start. Learning rate 3e-4 peak with linear decay to zero over the epoch, so the checkpoint is the annealed end of the run. The three Dr. Doom drafters it is compared with below are [itw, step 80,813](https://huggingface.co/atutej/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft-dr-doom_datakit-sft-mix_itw-80k-20261020), [itw, step 160,860](https://huggingface.co/atutej/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft-dr-doom_datakit-sft-mix_itw-160k-20261020) and [targetgen warm-started from the 80k checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/atutej/dspark_grug-67b-a2b-datakit-sft-dr-doom_datakit-sft-mix_targetgen-warm80k-20261020).
